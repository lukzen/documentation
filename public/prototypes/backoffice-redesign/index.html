Ergos · Backoffice Reorganization
Proposal demo — chips mark what moved (was: …) and what is new (new) · every ↗ is a new cross-link
What this prototype demonstrates
  1. Nav regroup — items grouped by job (Catalog · Bookings · Pricing · Finance · Partners); moved items keep a was: … chip.
  2. Link mesh — booking refs, agencies and pricing rules cross-link (marked ↗); the reservation detail is the hub.
  3. One agency page — Details · Employees · P&L · Credit tabs; the separate Employment nav item is folded in.
  4. Consistency system — row click → detail (kebab for secondary), one badge style, one currency format, one waterfall component (cascade = rules, waterfall = money, Brand not Chain).
