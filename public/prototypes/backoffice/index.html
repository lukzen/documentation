Payments & Settlement

Agency → Ergos

Balance settlements

An agency settles its full outstanding balance in a single payment (no partials, no per-booking allocation) — by corporate card (TropiPay, cleared immediately) or by ACH via Square — no fee to the agency: it pays the exact amount and Ergos absorbs Square's processing cost (≤$5). ACH settles in 2–3 business days — the balance stays Pending and credit is not freed until the ACH actually completes. A failed ACH leaves the balance unchanged and notifies the agency.

Story 10 · per-agency accounts

Credit accounts

One account per agency, granted or deposit-based — never both (grant actions are rejected on an account with deposits and vice versa). GRANTED: limit = Σgrants, not doubled — credit is just a number; owed = full utilization; the grant revolves via settlement and persists until revoked. DEPOSIT: limit = 2 × deposits (every deposit doubles; withdrawals un-double, floored at utilization); deposits spend first, drawdowns beyond them are debt. Paying with credit pays at confirm; on deposit-based accounts free-cancellation rates may overdraw up to min(25% of limit, $2,500) with a ≤72 h cover deadline (uncovered → sweep auto-cancels fee-free + strike); granted accounts are hard-capped — never overdraw. Utilization above the limit (open overdraft, or a revoke below utilization) freezes new bookings until settled. NR overdraft stays a per-agency privilege. Warn at 80% (global). Every change below is a ledger/audit row — nothing is a bare editable number.

Stories 3 & 6 · hourly sweep

Cover / payment deadlines — CONFIRMED_UNPAID bookings

"Pay later" is retired — paying with soft credit pays at confirm, so a row here is an overdraft cover obligation (deposit-based accounts only — granted accounts are hard-capped and never appear here, #188): a free-cancellation booking that drew beyond the account limit, confirmed with cover deadline = min(now + 72 h, free-cxl date − global buffer). Reminders (30 d / 7 d / 24 h) are computed at send time from the current deadline, so extending a deadline needs no rescheduling. Uncovered at the deadline → the hourly sweep auto-cancels fee-free inside the free-cancellation window, restores the drawdowns, and strikes the agency (audited creditHold) — no review queue.
